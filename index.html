Peer Feedback Organizer

Peer Feedback Organizer is a Google Sheets tool designed to help film students organize peer feedback on short-film and video projects.

What it does

The tool helps students import peer-feedback notes into a structured spreadsheet and manage those notes throughout the editing process.

  • Import pasted peer feedback into the current Google Sheet
  • Categorize feedback by note type
  • Assign priority levels
  • Track the status of individual notes
  • Filter feedback by priority, status, or note type

Google account access

Peer Feedback Organizer runs inside Google Sheets. It requests access to the current spreadsheet so it can read and update the feedback data needed for its features. It does not require access to unrelated spreadsheets in your Google account.

Privacy

For details about how Google user data is handled, read the Peer Feedback Organizer Privacy Policy.
