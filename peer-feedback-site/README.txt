Peer Feedback Organizer website

Files:
- index.html                 Homepage
- privacy/index.html         Privacy policy

Before publishing:
1. Replace YOUR-EMAIL@example.com in both HTML files with your real support/contact email.
2. Upload the files to the root of your GitHub Pages repository.
3. Enable GitHub Pages in Settings > Pages.
4. Add your custom domain in Settings > Pages.
5. Configure your domain's DNS records as instructed by GitHub.
6. Verify your domain in Google Search Console.
7. Use https://YOURDOMAIN.com/ as the OAuth Application home page.
8. Use https://YOURDOMAIN.com/privacy/ as the OAuth Privacy policy link.
